# 010.1 — Holidays + half-days

Market calendar: full holidays skip session; half-days adjust close/monitor end. Sessions on closed days are not scored as thesis losses.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Calendar module marks closed/half-day for injectable dates (unit-tested incl. known US holiday)
- [ ] Supervisor skips purchases on full holiday and sets appropriate session status (not infra_skip thesis blame)
- [ ] Half-day early close triggers Outcome at correct time in tests
